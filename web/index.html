03CONNECTIONS · WE CALL THIS DREAMING

See what might connect.

MAYA“Another note says the invitation has no start time.”

Pip: These notes share a topic. Astral can suggest a connection. You read the notes and decide whether to keep it.

A POSSIBLE CONNECTION

Questions about the time ↔ an invitation with no time.

A suggestion to review. You decide.

04CHECK LATER · YOUR RESEARCH LIST

Give a question a date.

MAYA“Will a clearer invitation actually help?”

Pip: Add that question to your review list. Choose when to come back, check what happened, and record what you found.

QUESTION TO CHECK

Did fewer people ask about the start time?

You choose a review date.
Today: a dated review list you manage. Automatic web research and alerts are planned.

Pip: Explore a realistic change or a playful idea in a separate worksheet. Your imagined possibilities stay separate from your original notes.

REALISTIC

Put the time at the top.

IMAGINATIVE

An invitation that speaks.

Today: a structured worksheet for thinking. Generated simulations, images, and video are planned.
