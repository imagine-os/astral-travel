# Lesson illustration art direction

The homepage teaches one action at a time through Maya and Pip. The six images were generated with the built-in image tool, then optimized to transparent WebP without changing the artwork. Exact lesson text remains accessible HTML beside each illustration.

## Visual continuity

Maya is a fictional adult with warm brown skin, short dark curls, plum glasses, a sky-blue jacket, cream shirt, indigo trousers, and coral sneakers. Pip is a small golden star with dark eyes and a lavender scarf. Blue means original notes, violet means ideas, and amber means hypothetical worlds. Transparent cutouts work on both light and dark backgrounds. All examples are fictional.

## Delivery

Assets live in `web/assets/lessons/`. Each is 1536 × 1024 pixels with alpha transparency. The first illustration was used as the character/style reference for every subsequent image. Do not bake labels into the artwork or imply that Pip is a live AI assistant. Pip is an illustrated guide for the local browser demo.
