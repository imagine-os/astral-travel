# A world worth entering

The identity is an optical-glass observatory suspended in navy space: cold blue evidence, violet interpretation, and amber imagination. The hero is symbolic artwork, not a rendering of an implemented spatial engine.

## Original artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this release; incorporated at `web/assets/observatory.webp` and `docs/assets/astral-banner.webp`.

Prompt: cinematic architectural memory observatory floating in deep navy space; luminous optical-glass torus and orbital rings centered-right, ice-blue and lilac lighting, warm-white core, orbiting archival slabs and sparse constellations. Left third reserved for headline space. Sophisticated museum-installation aesthetic, no text or logos. Balanced exposure and clear separation.

## Future motion brief for Fal or Higgsfield

Use the existing observatory artwork as the image reference. Produce a 6–8 second seamless, restrained loop: a nearly imperceptible orbital movement, subtle movement in the glass refractions, one traveling pulse of light along an archival orbit. Locked camera or a very slow 2% push. Preserve the composition and negative space exactly. No generated lettering, new objects, camera whip, flashes, or morphing geometry. Maintain exposure and readable contrast behind the HTML headline.

Deliver a clean 1920×1080 master and an optimized web version. Target a muted loop below 4 MB, with a WebP poster and reduced-motion fallback. Validate mobile decoding, responsive crop, page performance, and fallback before release. Video is optional atmosphere, not navigation. Connect providers on a server-side adapter; never ship provider secrets in the Pages client.

A 25-second follow-up should capture actual software: source capture (5s), connection discovery (6s), citation inspection (5s), scenario boundary (5s), export/MCP integration (4s). Every visible result must come from a reproducible example. Use editorial captions written and rendered outside the video generator. Do not animate fake model responses or invent customer logos, adoption counts, or benchmark scores.

## Evolving into spatial memory

Themes and layout manifests should point to stable memory IDs. A new style must not rewrite facts or their provenance. Source/processed/simulated states need textual labels as well as color. Voice, controller, keyboard, mouse, and pen should route through the same semantic action layer. The current release implements keyboard, pointer, and touch web controls; other modalities remain planned.
